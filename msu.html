01Which PhD? The two in plain terms

Recommended

PhD in Computer Science (CSE)

  • 30 credits of coursework (24+ at the 800 level) + at least 24 credits of CSE 999
  • 18 credits of breadth with at least one course each in System Design & Analysis, Theory & Algorithms and Data Analysis & Applications
  • Finish breadth by the end of semester 3 (with an MS) or semester 4 (without)
  • Attend 75% of colloquium and seminar talks in years 1–2
  • Qualifier, comprehensive exam (proposal) by end of year 4, final defense (typically by year 5)
  • PIs: Ross, Kong, Li, Boddeti
Option for driving perception

PhD in Electrical & Computer Engineering

  • 36 credits of coursework beyond the bachelor's, all at 800+, + at least 24 credits of ECE 999
  • Qualifier Part A: 3 core courses from 2+ areas, average ≥ 3.5, each ≥ 3.0
  • Qualifier Part B: a research project with a written report and oral exam. Both parts within 2 years.
  • One 800+ course outside Engineering (e.g. MTH, STT, PHY)
  • Attend 7 seminars in year 1. No 800/900 independent-study credits count.
  • PI: Morris

Can your UNCW master's count? Yes, as a waiver of coursework, not a lump-sum transfer.

MSU works differently from CSU. You don't transfer the degree; you ask to have requirements waived.

  • CSE PhD: "PhD students who earned a MS degree or its equivalent from outside MSU may receive permission to waive up to 24 credits of the 30 non-research credits." Request it "no later than the fifth week" of the PhD. Give your advisor your transcript and a syllabus for each course, with "a detailed description of topics covered and assigned textbooks". The Graduate Program Director decides which credits and breadth areas are waived.
  • College-wide (applies to ECE too): "PhD students may ask their guidance committee to consider graduate-level courses completed prior to the doctoral program." Approval is noted in your program plan, and formal transfer is "usually not necessary". ECE also lets up to 3 credits of master's thesis research count.
  • MS thesis bonus (CSE): if you write an MS thesis, you can apply within 2 weeks of starting to use it as the written part of the qualifying exam.
  • Do now: save a syllabus for every UNCW graduate course (topics + textbook). You'll need them in week 1.

Best case for the CSE PhD: waive 24 credits, take about 2 courses, and get the Theory & Algorithms requirement waived through your MS coursework. Confirm that last part (question 1 below).

05Professional doctorate

None found at MSU Engineering. The research PhD is the only doctorate.

The College of Engineering lists MS and PhD programs only. So the "would they find an employer for me?" question doesn't come up here: every MSU doctoral path is a funded research PhD. (Michigan's D.Eng programs are at UM–Ann Arbor and UM–Dearborn, both built for working professionals.)
